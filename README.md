A newspaper front page summing up Sunday 22 November 2026 in a single
image, drawn with PIL primitives (original vector artwork, no photos, no
downloaded images). **Early edition, drawn 02:30 UTC**: Hong Kong's
Election Committee vote, COP31 still without a deal in Antalya, the
Sparta II still ablaze off Finland, Saturday's Premier League results and
Sunday's fixtures (Liverpool v Man Utd at Anfield) — plus a DATE CHECK box
that names the lookalike stories deliberately left out because they are
not 22 November news (Norway's oil ruling, Ghana's banknotes, Kenya's
Ebola discharge, the Peru quake, the US midterms, Lagarde's resignation).

Running `draw.py` regenerates `entry.png` (1024×1024, ~196 KB);
`main.py` copies that file to the grader's output folder. Deterministic:
two runs write identical bytes. The footer states the date discipline —
every story on the page happened or was first reported on Sunday
22 November 2026.
